GLP1 Life privacy policy

Private testing draft · 20 October 2026

What we store

Your weigh-ins, injection log (only what you enter), workouts, meal plans, check-ins and settings are stored on your phone. If you turn on Cloud backup they are also stored in your private GLP1 Life cloud account (Supabase, EU), visible only to you.

If you post in the community, the text and any photo you attach are stored so other signed-in members can see them. A community photo is not kept in the private meal-photo folder.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect them, GLP1 Life reads your daily step count from Health Connect to show it on your Home screen. It does not read other Health Connect types. Writing a workout back to Health Connect is off in this version, and the Android app does not request that access. We never sell or share step data or use it for advertising. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings. If a later Android build asks to save a workout, this page will say so before that access is requested.

Camera

Camera video used for live coaching is processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Food photo diary

If you use the photo diary, your meal photos (resized to about 200 KB) are stored in a private folder in your GLP1 Life cloud account (Supabase, London) that only you can access. To estimate what's on the plate, your photo is sent securely from our server to Google's Gemini API on a paid plan. Under Google's paid-service terms (Gemini API terms), Google doesn't use it to improve its products or train its models, but may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. Please avoid including people or personal details in your photos. Results are estimates, not medical advice. Your diary entries and photos are included when you export your data and are permanently deleted when you delete a meal or your account. If you type a description of your meal, it is sent and handled in the same way as the photo (a description can also be checked on its own, without a photo). Up to 20 meal checks (photo or typed) a day per account.

Trainers

If you join with a personal trainer's code and agree to share, that trainer can view (not change) the items you chose. You can switch sharing off in Settings at any time.

Delete your account

In the app, Settings → Cloud backup → delete asks GLP1 Life to remove the cloud account it can reach, including meal photos and certificate images stored for that account. The confirmation says data kept only on the phone stays on the phone.

If you no longer have the app, email support@glp1life.co.uk from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. You do not need to install the app again to send that request.

This page is still a private testing draft. Before it is used on Google Play, the account owner needs to confirm exactly which server records the delete function removes, and how long Google keeps a meal photo or typed meal description sent for an estimate.

Your rights

Export or delete your cloud data any time in Settings → Cloud backup. Questions or data requests: support@glp1life.co.uk.

GLP1 Life does not give medical advice. Check with your GP or prescriber.
