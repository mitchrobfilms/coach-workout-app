GLP1 Life privacy policy

Private testing draft · 20 October 2026

What we store

Your weigh-ins, injection log (only what you enter), workouts, meal plans, check-ins and settings are stored on your phone. If you turn on Cloud backup they are also stored in your private GLP1 Life cloud account (Supabase, EU), visible only to you.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect them, GLP1 Life reads only your daily step count from Health Connect to show it on your Home screen. We never write to Health Connect, never read other data types, and never sell or share this data or use it for advertising. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Camera

Camera video used for live coaching is processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Food photo diary

If you use the photo diary, your meal photos (resized to about 200 KB) are stored in a private folder in your GLP1 Life cloud account (Supabase, London) that only you can access. To estimate what's on the plate, your photo is sent securely from our server to Google's Gemini API on a paid plan. Under Google's paid-service terms (Gemini API terms), Google doesn't use it to improve its products or train its models, but may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. Please avoid including people or personal details in your photos. Results are estimates, not medical advice. Your diary entries and photos are included when you export your data and are permanently deleted when you delete a meal or your account. Up to 20 photo checks a day per account.

Trainers

If you join with a personal trainer's code and agree to share, that trainer can view (not change) the items you chose. You can switch sharing off in Settings at any time.

Your rights

Export or delete your cloud data any time in Settings → Cloud backup. Questions or data requests: support@glp1life.co.uk.

GLP1 Life does not give medical advice. Check with your GP or prescriber.
