GLP1 Life trainer terms (draft)

Draft for private testing — to be reviewed before launch.

  1. You are an independent, suitably qualified and insured personal trainer.
  2. For each client who joins with your code you choose £2/month or £20/year. GLP1 Life invoices you monthly; no payments are processed in the app yet.
  3. Clients' health data is shared with you only with their explicit consent and only the items they choose. You must keep it confidential, use it only to coach that client, and stop using it when they withdraw consent (UK GDPR).
  4. You must not give medication or dosing advice — direct clients to their GP/prescriber.
  5. GLP1 Life may suspend your listing or code at any time.
